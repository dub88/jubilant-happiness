# Hollow Oak Bakery: notes from the owner

Website address will be https://www.hollowoakbakery.example (www is the main one).

**Who we are.** Hollow Oak Bakery, 14 Mill Lane, Cedarbrook CB4 2QT. Phone 01632 960142. Email hello@hollowoakbakery.example.

**Hours.** Tuesday to Saturday 7am to 3pm. Sunday 8am to 1pm. Closed Mondays.

**What we sell.**
- Sourdough loaf (white, seeded or rye): £4.80
- Baguette: £2.20
- Cinnamon bun: £3.10
- Croissant: £2.60, almond croissant £3.20
- Sausage roll (pork from Dyer's farm up the road): £3.90
- Filter coffee £2.50, flat white £3.40
- Celebration cakes to order, from £45, a week's notice please

**About, in my words.** Me (Ines Carvalho) and my brother Tomas opened in 2017 in what used to be the village post office. We mill some of our own flour from grain grown at Dyer's farm. Everything is baked that morning; what's left at closing goes to the food bank on Chapel Street. We're not fancy. People come for the seeded sourdough and stay to chat.
